# Company
ASR Global Solutions is a startup B2B solutions company helping businesses source products, streamline procurement and keep operations moving. The company focuses on reliable communication, right-fit sourcing and responsive support.

## Supply chain solutions
ASR Global Solutions currently offers product sourcing, vendor coordination, bulk procurement and delivery support for companies across industries. Requirements are evaluated based on product specifications, quantity, budget, delivery location and timeline. Product availability, pricing and delivery schedules must be confirmed through a formal enquiry.

## Working process
First, the customer shares the product, quantity, specifications, delivery location and required timeline. Next, ASR evaluates sourcing options and provides a proposal. After approval, ASR coordinates the supply and keeps the customer informed through delivery.

## Construction
Construction solutions are planned for the future and are not currently offered. Planned areas include construction material supply, project procurement support, and contractor and vendor coordination.
